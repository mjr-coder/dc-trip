For a Capitol photo, use the east plaza before or after the tour. The Rotunda and Statuary Hall are generally part of the guided route.

Allow about 25–30 minutes on foot from Bistro Cacao to Natural History, or take a short ride if lunch runs long and about 2 minutes from Clyde’s to the arena. The arena generally prohibits bags, with exceptions including 4 × 6 in wallet-size clutches.

Budget · USD

Trip essentials

Weather & packing

Historical late-November conditions around DC are roughly mid-50s °F by day and upper-30s °F overnight. Sunset is around 16:49 local time.

Pack a wind-resistant layer, warm midlayer, comfortable shoes, compact rain protection and a power bank. Check the forecast close to departure.

Clima e mala

Historicamente, o fim de novembro em DC costuma ter máximas por volta de 12–14 °C e mínimas perto de 3–5 °C. O sol se põe por volta das 16h49.

Leve corta-vento, camada quente, sapatos confortáveis, proteção compacta para chuva e bateria portátil. Confira a previsão perto da saída.
